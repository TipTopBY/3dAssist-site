2. What 3dAssist is

3dAssist searches public 3D model catalogs, prepares a print plan for a selected model file, and sends the prepared job to a 3D printer you have configured on your own local network. Print planning and slicing run on a 3dAssist backend whose address you configure in the application. Printing itself happens on your own hardware, under your control.

3dAssist is provided as-is and is under active development. Features, supported printers and supported model sources may change or be removed between releases.
